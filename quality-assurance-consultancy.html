Problems It Solves

Clear support for common quality assurance and compliance problems.

Many providers have quality processes in place, but the challenge is making them consistent, visible, evidence-based and useful for improvement.

IQA sampling is inconsistent

Sampling may happen, but records, rationale, feedback and follow-up actions are not always clear or consistent.

Standardisation lacks impact

Meetings can become routine without enough evidence of assessor development, shared decisions or improved practice.

Actions are hard to track

Quality findings need clear owners, deadlines, evidence and follow-up so improvement does not get lost.

Documentation feels fragmented

Assessment, IQA, learner support, staff CPD and quality improvement records may sit separately without a clear quality story.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered

These FAQs are written for providers and quality leads who need to understand whether this support fits their QA and improvement needs.

Can you review our IQA system?

Yes. Support can include sampling plans, IQA records, assessor feedback, action logs, standardisation evidence and follow-up processes.

Can existing quality documents be reviewed?

Yes. Existing policies, records, trackers and reports can be reviewed for clarity, consistency, evidence gaps and usability.

Can this help with compliance readiness?

Yes. Support can help teams organise evidence, identify risk areas and prepare clearer records for internal review or external scrutiny.

Can you support assessors and IQAs?

Yes. Workshops or consultancy can help teams understand sampling, feedback, standardisation, assessor support and action tracking.

Can support be delivered online?

Yes. Support can be delivered online, in-house or through a blended consultancy model depending on your documents and team needs.

Can support be tailored to our qualification?

Yes. QA support can be shaped around your awarding body requirements, unit structure, learner group, delivery model and internal processes.
