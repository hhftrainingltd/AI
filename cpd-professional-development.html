Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered

These FAQs are written for decision-makers who need to understand whether the CPD is suitable for their staff or organisation.

Can the CPD be tailored to our organisation?

Yes. Content, examples, activities and outcomes can be adapted to your sector, staff roles, organisational priorities and delivery needs.

Is the CPD accredited?

Accreditation and certificate arrangements must be confirmed for the selected programme. The catalogue presents CPD course titles and does not itself establish awarding body approval or regulated qualification status.

Can this support annual staff development?

Yes. Sessions can support annual CPD plans, refresher training, induction, team development days and targeted improvement priorities.

Can leaders receive a separate briefing?

Yes. Leadership briefings can focus on staff development priorities, implementation, quality improvement and organisational planning.

Can this be delivered online?

Yes. CPD can be delivered in-house, live online or as a hybrid programme depending on your team location and schedule.

Do staff receive practical takeaways?

Yes. Sessions can include action points, discussion tasks, resources, reflection prompts and next steps for workplace application.
