AI CPD · Available to discussAI for Teaching, Learning and AssessmentUse AI to support lesson preparation, learning resources and feedback while keeping educators responsible for accuracy and assessment decisions.View course detailsHide course details

Who it is for

Teachers, trainers, lecturers, assessors and learning teams.

What the course covers

  • AI foundations, prompting and checking generated material
  • Lesson planning, resource adaptation and inclusive learning activities
  • Feedback preparation, assessment authenticity and learner guidance
  • Information handling, copyright awareness and organisational AI policies

Practical activity

Develop a lesson outline and an assessment activity using a fictional teaching scenario. Check subject accuracy, accessibility, alignment and learner ownership before approving the resources.

What participants can develop

A reviewed teaching resource, a reusable prompt and a checklist for appropriate AI use in teaching and assessment.

Responsible professional practice

Educators check all content. AI does not replace subject expertise, safeguarding procedures or assessment judgement.

CPD course questions

Can the CPD be designed around our organisation’s requirements?

Yes. Courses and learning pathways can be designed or adapted around your organisation’s sector, staff roles, learning needs, workplace scenarios and delivery requirements. Agree the course mix, outcomes, accessibility support, assessment expectations and certificate arrangements with HHF before delivery.

What does the Level 2 CPD designation mean?

The course titles reproduce the Level 2 wording in the HHF catalogue. The catalogue does not establish regulated qualification status, awarding body approval or licence eligibility. Confirm the status and certificate wording for your chosen course with HHF before booking.

How are the CPD courses delivered?

The catalogue describes face-to-face, remote and blended delivery. Confirm the format, timetable, duration, accessibility support, assessment expectations and evidence of completion with HHF for the selected course.

Which courses should an organisation start with?

The first course in each of the six subject groups is identified as a suggested flagship course. Use these as a planning starting point, then select courses against staff roles, existing knowledge, priority risks and learning needs.

How do the foundation AI courses connect to specialist learning?

The proposed pathway begins with eight accessible AI foundation short courses, progresses to applied workplace skills and then branches into professional and technical specialisms. Progression and any formal level designation must be agreed for the chosen programme.

Are course prices and durations fixed?

The supplied catalogue does not specify prices or teaching hours. Ask HHF to confirm fees, duration, entry support, assessment and certificate arrangements for your selected course and delivery format.

Course selection and current guidance

Use the official regulated qualification register when formal qualification recognition is required. For organisational practice, consult NCSC phishing guidance and ICO guidance on AI and data protection alongside your own procedures.
