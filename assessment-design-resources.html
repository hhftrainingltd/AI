Problems It Solves

Clear support for common assessment design and evidence problems.

Many providers know what needs to be assessed, but the challenge is turning criteria into valid tasks, usable documents and clear evidence trails.

Assessment tasks are too generic

Tasks may not capture authentic performance, practical understanding or enough evidence for reliable assessor judgement.

Mapping is unclear

Teams may struggle to show exactly where each learning outcome, assessment criterion and evidence source is covered.

Evidence tracking is inconsistent

Without clear trackers, learner evidence can become difficult to follow, sample, improve or explain during review.

AI creates authenticity concerns

Written evidence alone may not be enough. Providers need stronger professional discussion, observation and practical evidence routes.

Frequently Asked Questions

Your questions, answered

These FAQs are written for providers and quality leads who need to understand whether this support fits their assessment needs.

Can you design a full assessment pack?

Yes. Support can include learner instructions, assessor guidance, marking records, mapping documents, feedback templates and evidence tracking tools.

Can existing assessment documents be reviewed?

Yes. Existing resources can be reviewed for clarity, validity, mapping, evidence gaps, authenticity risks and quality assurance usability.

Can this support AI-resilient assessment?

Yes. Tasks can be redesigned to include practical evidence, observation, professional discussion and stronger authenticity checks.

Can you support assessors and IQAs?

Yes. Workshops or consultancy can help teams understand task design, feedback, mapping, sampling and standardisation expectations.

Can support be delivered online?

Yes. Support can be delivered online, in-house or through a blended consultancy model depending on your documents and team needs.

Can resources be tailored to our qualification?

Yes. Resources can be shaped around your awarding body requirements, unit structure, learner group, delivery model and internal processes.
